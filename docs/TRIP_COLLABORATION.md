# Trip Collaboration Checklist

- Confirm who owns a trip and who can collaborate.
- Validate access before showing private trip details.
- Check permissions for edits and deletions.
- Test what happens when a collaborator is removed.
- Keep invitation and sharing errors clear.
- Verify changes with owner, collaborator, and unauthorized-user scenarios.

Sharing behavior should be enforced by the backend, not only by the interface.
